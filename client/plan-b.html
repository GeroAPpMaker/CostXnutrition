Creamy Chicken Pasta | Food Cost & Nutrition Activity | Use this sheet if the app is unavailable.

Your Scenario

You run a small café. Creamy Chicken Pasta sells for ₱200 per plate.

Management wants a food cost of 30% or less. That means cost per portion must be ₱60.00 or less.

The dietitian wants each portion at:

  • Calories: 650 kcal or less
  • Sodium: 700 mg or less
  • Protein: 30 g or more

Your goal: edit the recipe to hit all four targets. One batch serves 4 portions.

Formula Card

Ingredient cost = Quantity (g or ml) × Price (₱ per 100g/ml) ÷ 100

Total recipe cost = Sum of all ingredient costs

Cost per portion = Total recipe cost ÷ 4 (portions)

Food cost % = Cost per portion ÷ Selling price × 100

Target selling price = Cost per portion ÷ Target food cost % (as a decimal)

Example: Cost per portion = ₱60. Target = 30%. Selling price = ₱60 ÷ 0.30 = ₱200.

Calories per portion = Sum of (Quantity × kcal per 100g/ml) ÷ 4
